## What it is

Cloud Pet is a DIY handheld virtual pet in a soft, cloud-shaped enclosure. Its translucent lavender-blue shell pairs a square colour display with a dark circular bezel and three rounded buttons, with a larger centre button.

Inside are a Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3, a 1.3-inch IPS display, a rechargeable battery, and custom circuit boards. USB-C provides charging and programming, while an integrated lanyard loop makes it easy to carry.

## Why it is worth making

This project brings together electronics, custom PCB design, and 3D printing in a small object with plenty of personality. It is a starting point for creating your own digital companion, experimenting with pixel art, and developing pet behaviours and simple games.

The enclosure uses screw-fastened parts, supported electronics, and locking display connectors to keep the assembly secure and accessible.

## What it is like to use

The rounded body is designed to sit comfortably in your hand, with three tactile buttons below the display. The translucent shell gives it a playful look, while the programmable hardware leaves room to make the pet your own.
